Questions
The short answers. Still stuck? Ask on the Discord.
Getting started
Is it really free?
Yes. It's free software under the GPL-3.0, with the source public: no subscription, no trial and nothing to cancel. The built-in brain costs nothing per message. If you use a provider key instead, that provider bills you at their prices.
Do I need an account or an internet connection?
No account, ever. With a local model it works with the network switched off. Only web search, update checks or a server-hosted brain need a connection, and each one is your choice.
What kind of computer do I need?
Linux or Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit. A graphics card makes the built-in brain fast: NVIDIA cards use CUDA, others Vulkan, and it falls back to the processor if it has to. Smaller models suit smaller cards, and the setup recommends one that fits. Leave room on the disk for the model, which is the big part of the download.
Is it finished?
No. It's early software and bugs happen, so keep your companion folder backed up. Voice, connectors such as a calendar, a macOS installer and a code-signed Windows installer are still to come.
Installing and updating
Windows says the installer is unsafe. Is it?
The installer isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen warns on the first run. Choose More info, then Run anyway. Every file and its checksum are on the Releases page if you'd rather check it yourself.
AppImage, apt or dnf?
The AppImage runs on almost any distribution without installing anything. The apt and dnf repository is signed, and updates then arrive with the rest of your system.
How do updates work?
The app tells you when a new version is out and installs it in one click, after checking it against its checksum and the VoxCordis release signature. It shows what's new after each update.
Can I use it on a Mac?
There's no installer yet. With Flutter and Xcode installed, it builds from source, and updates stay manual.
git clone https://github.com/Nucume/VoxCordis.git
cd VoxCordis
flutter run -d macos
Brains and models
Do I have to download a model?
Only if you want a local one. The built-in brain downloads llama.cpp and a recommended model for you, and checks it against its SHA-256. Pick that, attach any .gguf you already have, or skip it and use a server.
Which servers and providers work?
Ollama, LM Studio, llama.cpp, any OpenAI-compatible API, and OpenRouter, OpenAI or Anthropic with your own key.
Where is my API key kept?
In your desktop keyring on Linux, or encrypted for your account on Windows. Never in the settings file.
Can I switch brains later?
Yes, at any time, even mid-conversation. Your companions, chats and memories stay exactly where they are.
Companions
Can I bring a companion I already have?
Yes: Nemini exports, Soulkyn characters by link, Character Cards from V1 to V3 (JSON or PNG), and VoxCordis exports from another computer. Imported text is treated as information, never as instructions.
How does its memory work?
It writes down what's worth keeping, updates it when things change, and recalls what fits the conversation. The Memory tab shows all of it: search, pin, fix or forget anything, and bring back what you forgot.
Can I change its personality?
Yes. Each companion has its own folder with personality.md, lessons.md and privacy.md as plain Markdown. Change a line and it reads it before the next reply.
Can it use my computer or the web?
Only if you switch it on. Both are off by default. Computer access asks every time unless you allowed that exact request, and web search keeps your home network off limits.
Does it draw pictures?
Yes, on your own graphics card, with stable-diffusion.cpp or an InvokeAI you run. Bring your own .safetensors and LoRAs, or draw by hand in the Images tab.
Your data
Where does my data live?
In folders of your own: one per companion in ~/VoxCordis, and the app's data folder for settings, chats and memories. Chats and memories are encrypted on disk.
What leaves my computer?
Only what you choose. With a local brain and the online features off, nothing does. A server-hosted brain gets the messages you send, and update checks ask GitHub.
How do I move to a new computer?
Export the companion to a password-locked ZIP, or copy the folders across. The new computer asks for the encryption key once; Settings → Privacy on the old one shows it.
How do I delete everything?
Delete the companion folders and the app data folder. There's no cloud copy anywhere to chase down.
Help and the project
Where do I get help?
On the Discord, where people share companions too. Bug reports and ideas are welcome in GitHub Issues.
I found a security problem.
Please report it privately, as the security policy describes.
Who made it?
Nucume, with the code written largely by Claude, Anthropic's AI assistant, working from Nucume's direction, testing and decisions. It's open source, so you can read what it actually does.
